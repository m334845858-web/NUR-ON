NETLIFY FORM / GMAIL SETUP:
The form name is: nureon-design-enquiry
After publishing:
1. Open Netlify dashboard > your site > Forms and verify the form appears.
2. Open Site configuration > Notifications > Emails and webhooks > Form submission notifications.
3. Add mhrafiservies2025@gmail.com as the notification recipient for nureon-design-enquiry.
4. Submit a test enquiry and confirm it appears under Forms and reaches the inbox (check Spam too).

The email address is intentionally NOT exposed in the public website source.
This ZIP is a deployable site package; generating it does not itself publish the site.
The trouser visuals and campaign illustration are original vector concept art, not photographs or approved production technical drawings. Replace them with final owner-approved design images before presenting them as production-ready products.
